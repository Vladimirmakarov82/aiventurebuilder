01 · Why now

Borders got higher

Pain 1

Moving money and people got harder

Every year brings new limits:

  • Transfers abroad take weeks and get stuck
  • Banks close accounts without explanation
  • Fewer direct flights, longer routes
  • Visa refusals and queues at consulates
  • Residence and citizenship rules change every year
  • Capital needs protection in several jurisdictions
Pain 2

Deadlines stay the same

Tuition, surgery, a deal, a family event: the date is set, the route changes every month
